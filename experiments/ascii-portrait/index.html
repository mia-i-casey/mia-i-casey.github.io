PORTRAIT / CHARACTER STUDY

A portrait, with room to change

What happens when the surface of an image becomes part of the interaction?

This study starts with an ASCII portrait. As the pointer moves, a narrow horizontal strip shifts sideways, with scattered fragments close by. Cyan, blue, pink and amber briefly interrupt the black-and-white image. The rest of the portrait remains still, and each glitch fades within about a second.

Movement gives it form

The work explores a small tension between recognition and abstraction: a familiar image that briefly becomes something less fixed. It responds to the visitor’s movement instead of playing on a loop.

Behind the surface

A transparent portrait texture and a Three.js shader translate pointer movement into displaced scanlines. Each glitch has a fixed local area and a short lifetime. The renderer pauses when the image settles, and a still portrait remains available when motion is disabled.
